01 / ABOUT

Beyond showcasing tech—
make it work for real.

The challenge centers on real field problems, student-built Agentic AI solutions, and deployment validation. University students nationwide are invited to build intelligent agents that can actually run in campus or industry settings.

The challenge is organized under the Ministry of Education's NAPAI program, led by Prof. Chia-Hui Chang of National Central University, in collaboration with TAAI 2026 and coordinated by co-PI Prof. Min-Yuh Day of National Taipei University.

CAMPUS IS AN IDEAL AGENTIC AI TESTBED

Campuses offer bounded yet authentic settings across academic affairs, libraries, learning support, administration, and student services. Students are both developers and users, enabling fast feedback and continuous iteration.

04 / JUDGING

TWO STAGES・FROM PROPOSAL TO LIVE VALIDATION

Judged not only on ideas,
but deployment and autonomy.

Preliminary｜Proposal deck + concept video

Deployment feasibility 25%

Whether the problem reflects a real need and the solution can run in a campus or industry field.

Technical plan completeness 25%

Clarity and feasibility of the Agentic architecture, tool use, and system integration plan.

Innovation 25%

Originality and potential to create meaningful improvement in the target field.

Generalizability 25%

Potential to extend the solution to comparable fields, workflows, or user scenarios.

Final｜Live Demo Poster + on-stage pitch

Implementation completeness 35%

A working agent demonstrated live and its realistic application potential.

Technical depth and autonomy 20%

Autonomous planning, tool use, integration, and multi-step task completion.

Validation and practical value 20%

Evidence of deployment, adoption, sustainable operation, and service or business value.

Innovation 15%

Novel insight and breakthrough contribution to the target field.

Presentation 10%

Completeness, clarity, and ability to engage judges and the audience.

A hands-on live demo is a core final requirement. If a finalist cannot provide an actual demo, the organizer reserves the final right to request the return of the finalist award.

09 / FAQ

Before you enter

Who can join and how large is a team?＋

Currently enrolled college and university students, including graduate students, may enter. Teams have 3–5 students, must name one leader, may be cross-campus and cross-disciplinary, and may optionally have one faculty adviser.

What is required for the preliminary round?＋

The draft schedule specifies a 20-page presentation deck and a 6-minute video, judged on deployment feasibility, technical plan completeness, innovation, and generalizability.

Is a live demo mandatory in the final?＋

Yes. The final uses a Live Demo Poster plus on-stage presentation. The organizer reserves the right to request return of the finalist award if an actual demo cannot be provided.

What support is provided?＋

Agentic AI expert sessions, cloud and model API resources, de-identified datasets, API documents, and one-on-one field mentoring for finalists.

What awards and opportunities are available?＋

Top and merit prizes, corporate special awards, certificates, digital badges, possible field adoption reviews, and priority internship interviews.
